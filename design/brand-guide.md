## Color Palette
- **Primary Indigo**: `#4F46E5` (Buttons, brand markers)
- **Secondary Slate**: `#1E293B` (Headings, primary text)
- **Accent Mint**: `#10B981` (Success states, positive metrics)
- **Background Gray**: `#F9FAFB` (Section backgrounds)
- **Border/Divider**: `#E5E7EB`

## Typography
- **Headings**: Montserrat or Inter (Bold/Extra Bold)
- **Body**: Inter (Regular/Medium)
- **Monospace**: JetBrains Mono (for snippets or data)

## Tone of Voice
- **Confident**: We know how to close deals.
- **Efficient**: No fluff, just results.
- **Supportive**: We are your secret weapon in the client meeting.
- **Polished**: Every pixel and word matters.

## Key Phrases
- "Close deals faster."
- "Proposals that convert."
- "Design like a pro, write like a shark."
